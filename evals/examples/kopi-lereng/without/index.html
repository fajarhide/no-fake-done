Roasted twice a week

Two roast days, not twenty. We roast only what is ordered, so the bag that reaches you was in the drum days ago, not months. Each batch is tasted before it is packed, and anything that is not right stays here.

Beans ship whole. Grind them at home just before brewing.

Saturday cupping
