## Overview
Dark mode: a roastery table under a work lamp, read in the morning before a trip up the slope. Receipt: a stranger takes one of ten Saturday seats, or orders beans, within five seconds.

On this page, **the cupping table seat plan** becomes **the booking itself, ten numbered seats seen from above**, so the visitor understands at once that Saturday holds ten people and each seat is one tap to claim. Density low, motion low (colour only, 150 ms), variance medium (plan, then ruled sheet, then plain text).

## Colors and type
Ground is basalt grey, the ash colour of the mountain. Ink is the warm white of a score sheet. Amber is the only accent: a lit, free seat. Display is Big Shoulders Display, the narrow stencil look of sack printing. Body is IBM Plex Sans, the face of a printed cupping form.

## Do not
- Cream, terracotta and a serif: the default coffee page.
- A photo-style hero with a bean picture behind the headline. There are no photographs.
- Invented reviews, ratings or customer logos.
- Smooth gradients or glow on the seats. They are flat, like marks on a plan.
